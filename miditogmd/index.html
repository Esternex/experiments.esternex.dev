Convert MIDI to GMD Online

This free MIDI to GMD converter transforms MIDI files into GMD format directly inside your browser. The conversion runs locally using WebAssembly, meaning your MIDI files are processed on your device instead of being uploaded to a server.

The tool is designed for musicians, producers, and developers who need to convert MIDI drum patterns and General MIDI data into GMD-compatible files quickly.

How to convert MIDI files to GMD

  1. Select a MIDI file using the upload area above.
  2. Wait while the browser converts the MIDI data.
  3. Download your generated GMD file.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a GMD file?

A GMD File is a geometry dash level file, this converter converts the midi file into a geometry dash level with the song played out in SFX triggers

Is this MIDI to GMD converter free?

Yes. The converter is free to use and works directly in your browser. No desktop software installation is required.

Are MIDI files uploaded anywhere?

No. Conversion happens locally inside your browser. Your MIDI files stay on your device.

What MIDI files are supported?

The converter supports standard MIDI files with .mid and .midi extensions.
